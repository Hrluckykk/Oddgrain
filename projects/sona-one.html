Questions, answered.

SONA is a modern audio brand focused on design-conscious, everyday wireless earbuds. Our flagship, SONA ONE, is built around comfort, clarity, and simplicity.

Consider how you listen most — commuting, focusing, or calls — and choose the model that fits your routine. SONA ONE is our all-rounder for everyday use.

SONA ONE is offered in Obsidian, Ivory, and Olive. Availability may vary by region.

Keep the earbuds and case clean and dry, store them in the case when not in use, and avoid prolonged exposure to moisture or extreme heat. Detailed care guidance will be included with your product.

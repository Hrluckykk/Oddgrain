02 / Why Oddgrain?

Perfectly
imperfect.

Grain is texture. It is proof that something has a surface, a history, a little resistance. Oddgrain is about leaving room for that.

The internet has enough things that look like everything else. I’m interested in the slightly strange idea, the unexpected detail, the rough edge that makes a good thing feel human.
